About Us

Cooking for your biggest days

Gani Catering serves Belagavi and surrounding areas with complete wedding and function catering — from welcome mocktails to live tandoor counters and dessert spreads.

Every order is taken personally — call, WhatsApp, or send an enquiry below and we'll work out a menu and headcount that fits your event and budget.

What we bring

Chaats & live counters, soups & salads, biryani & rice, tandoor, curries, breads, and a full sweets & dessert spread — veg and non-veg, sized for your guest count.
